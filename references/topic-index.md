# Topic index

Where to look for common themes. These are **pointers, not quotes**. They
come from general knowledge of the texts and may be off by a number or two,
so always run the lookup script and confirm the exact words and location
before quoting. Add to this file as you confirm good passages.

Installed texts: Marcus (Long, preferred; Casaubon), Epictetus (Long
selection + Enchiridion; Crossley's Golden Sayings), Seneca (Gummere's
Letters; Stewart's Minor Dialogues and On Benefits), Cleanthes' Hymn.

Confirmed passages (found with the script during testing):
- Anger: Seneca, Of Anger II.29 ("The greatest remedy for anger is delay")
- Difficult people: Marcus, Meditations 2.1 ("Begin the morning by saying to thyself…")
- Can't get up: Marcus, Meditations 5.1
- Loss: Epictetus, Enchiridion 11 ("I have restored it")
- Retreat into yourself: Marcus, Meditations 4.3
- Judgments, not things: Marcus, Meditations 8.47
- Revenge: Marcus, Meditations 6.6
- Resilience: Marcus, Meditations 4.49 (the promontory)
- Others' opinions: Epictetus, Discourses, "On Anxiety"
- Time: Seneca, Letter 1 ("On Saving Time")
- Noise and distraction: Seneca, Letter 56 ("On Quiet and Study")

Search tips: use two or three different keywords, and try the author filter,
e.g. `python3 scripts/find_passage.py "judgment" --file marcus`.

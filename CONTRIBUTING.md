# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve Stoa. Good contributions include:

- **Confirmed passages** for `references/topic-index.md`: the exact book,
  chapter, or letter, and the search words that find it with
  `scripts/find_passage.py`.
- **Practices** for `references/practices.md`, with a source in the primary
  texts and a plain description in your own words.
- **Bug fixes** to the scripts.
- **Example conversations** for the README.

## Ground rules

1. **Primary sources only for quotes.** Quotes must come from public-domain
   translations. Do not add text from copyrighted modern books; paraphrase
   and credit the author instead.
2. **Never commit personal data.** `journal/situations.md` is git-ignored.
   Do not add anyone's private notes or conversations.
3. **Keep the guardrails.** Changes must not weaken the crisis rule or the
   "not therapy" disclaimer.
4. **Plain language.** Write for readers who are new to Stoicism.

## How to submit a change

Fork the repo, make your change on a branch, and open a pull request with a
short description of what and why.
